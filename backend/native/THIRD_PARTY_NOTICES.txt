The libvips shared libraries are kept as separate JAR resources rather than
linked into the PhotoLib wrapper. They can be replaced by rebuilding with an
ABI-compatible package after updating the pinned version, filenames and both
archive checksums in native/build-native.ps1 and native/build-native.sh.

The build downloads pinned archives and verifies their SHA-256 digests.
libjpeg-turbo and stb are statically linked into the PhotoLib wrapper. The
platform-specific libvips shared library is packaged beside that wrapper in
the JAR and is extracted and preloaded by the Java runtime.
